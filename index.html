Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats does Stickerly support? ▼

Stickerly supports PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WEBP image formats up to 20MB in size.

What is the size of exported WhatsApp stickers? ▼

All exported stickers are rendered at exactly 512 × 512 pixels in standard PNG format, which is required by WhatsApp.

Does Stickerly upload my private photos? ▼

No. All editing, rendering, cropping, and PNG generation happen locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas technology.

How do I add downloaded stickers into WhatsApp? ▼

After downloading the 512x512 PNG, open WhatsApp on Android or iOS, go to any chat, tap the Sticker icon, and tap "+" to import or select from photo gallery.
